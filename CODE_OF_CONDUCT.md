# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of participation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Discuss the work and its effects rather than attacking a person.
- Accept correction, acknowledge mistakes, and help others participate safely.
- Protect private information and follow the repository's security-reporting boundary.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, deliberate
misrepresentation, and publishing another person's private information without permission
are not acceptable. The same standard applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, code,
review comments, and project-related public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Repository owners may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Sensitive reports should use
GitHub's private
[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/semantic-craft/famotype-macos/security/advisories/new)
flow with the title `Code of conduct report`; do not disclose private details in a public
issue. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and promptly as practical, with a response
proportionate to the conduct and its impact.

Good-faith disagreement about technical or project decisions is not itself a violation.
Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.
